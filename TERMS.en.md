## 1. What Malipin is

Malipin is a free service that suggests fashion and home products from online shops, matching
your Pinterest style or a photo you upload. You don't need an account with us to use it.

**Malipin doesn't sell anything itself.** When you click a product, you go to that shop. A
purchase contract is made only between you and that shop, on its terms. The shop is responsible
for delivery, returns, warranty and payment.

## 2. Advertising and affiliate links

Many links on Malipin are affiliate links. If you buy something through them, the shop pays us a
commission. The price stays the same for you. These links are marked "Werbung" (advertising). As
an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases.

Links to Vinted, willhaben and Depop are plain search links that we earn nothing from.

## 3. Prices, availability and suggestions

Prices, availability, images and descriptions come from the shops and affiliate networks and are
updated regularly. They can still change at any time. **The information in the shop is always
what counts.** Prices converted into other currencies are approximate.

Mali's suggestions are made automatically, partly with image recognition. They can be off. Please
check size, material and description in the shop before you buy.

## 4. Pinterest connection

When you connect your Pinterest account, Malipin only gets read access to your profile, boards
and pins. We don't post or change anything in your account. You can disconnect at any time.
Pinterest's own terms also apply.

## 5. Photo search

Only upload photos you are allowed to use. If a photo shows other people, you need their
permission. Photos with illegal, offensive or sexual content are not allowed. Uploaded photos are
only used briefly in memory for recognition and are not stored.

The "share your find" image is created only in your browser. You decide what you share and where.
